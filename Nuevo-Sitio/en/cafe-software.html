FAQ

Questions from café owners

Is a system worth it for a small café?

Yes, especially locally: pick-up orders, a digital loyalty card and a strong Google Maps profile cost little compared with a customer who comes back every week. We start with only the modules that add value.

Do I need a loyalty app?

No. Your customers don't download anything: the stamp card lives in WhatsApp or the browser and adds up automatically with each purchase.

How do pick-up orders work?

Customers choose from the QR or web menu, pay and pick a time. The bar gets the order on a screen or tablet and the customer gets a WhatsApp notice when it's ready.

Can you help me sell whole-bean coffee?

Yes. We set up a simple shop with delivery or pick-up, and tell the coffee's story (origin, farm, process) with photography and promotion in-store and on social.

Can it take table bookings?

Yes: tables for groups, work meetings, tastings and workshops, with automatic WhatsApp confirmation and reminders.
