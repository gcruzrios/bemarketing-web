FAQ

What people ask before the demo

Is BeMarketing an agency or a software company?

Both, in that order: we implement and run the software that organizes your bookings, operations and reporting, then amplify it with hospitality-specialized marketing. One team accountable for bringing in more bookings and for operations that can serve them well.

Do I have to replace the system I already use?

Not necessarily. If your PMS, POS or channel manager works, we integrate it and build on top. If it doesn't exist or holds you back, we implement our solution and migrate your data.

How long does implementation take?

Most businesses are live within 2 weeks: booking engine, WhatsApp assistant and KPI dashboard. Deeper integrations (inventory, accounting, multiple locations) are planned in phases.

What about bookings that come through Booking, Expedia or other platforms today?

You keep receiving them, synced to avoid overbooking. The goal isn't to switch channels off, but to have a growing share of guests book direct, commission-free.

How do you measure results?

With a day-one baseline and a shared dashboard: direct vs. OTA bookings, commissions paid, response time, occupancy or covers, average ticket and rating. Real, unedited data.

In a 30-minute demo we show you what your business looks like with direct bookings, organized operations and clear reports.
