The challenge for cafés

What holds a good café back

The competition is a block away. The difference comes from neighborhood visibility, the experience and the habit of coming back.

Many neighbors and people who work nearby walk past the shop, or search for it on Maps, without knowing it.

What we do: we show up in “coffee near me” with photos, hours and the attributes people look for: Wi-Fi, power outlets, pet friendly.

Frequently asked questions

Café FAQs

Is digital marketing worth it for a small café?

Yes, especially locally: showing up on Google Maps, having good photos and a list of regular customers costs little compared with what a customer who returns every week brings in.

How much does digital marketing for a café cost?

It depends on what you need: optimizing a Google profile is not the same as producing weekly content and running ads. After the audit we send you a proposal with a clear scope and cost, with no commitment.

How do I get customers to come back?

With a consistent experience and a reason to return: a loyalty card, weekly news via WhatsApp and seasonal products that sell out.

Do I need a loyalty app?

Not at the start. A stamp card and a well-managed WhatsApp list are usually enough, and easier for your customers to adopt.

Can you help me sell whole-bean coffee?

Yes. We work on the coffee’s story (origin, farm, process), product photography and promotion in the shop and on social media. If you want to sell online, we help you define the simplest channel.
