The challenge for hotels

Three problems we see in almost every hotel

Booking platforms bring visibility, but they charge for every guest. And occupancy swings a lot between seasons.

01

Too much dependence on OTAs

If most bookings come through Booking or Expedia, every night sold pays a commission and the guest’s data stays on the platform.

What we do: we showcase the hotel on Google Hotels and Maps, and give guests a reason to book on your own website.

02

Empty rooms in the green season

From May to November international tourism drops, and the usual response is to cut rates everywhere.

What we do: we prepare packages and campaigns for domestic tourism, long weekends and mid-year vacations.

03

Guests who compare and leave

A slow website, outdated photos or a WhatsApp that takes hours to reply make guests book somewhere else.

What we do: we improve photos, speed and response time so the booking closes on the first contact.

Frequently asked questions

Hotel FAQs

Can you help me depend less on Booking and Expedia?

Yes. It’s not about leaving the OTAs, which still bring visibility, but about getting a growing share of guests to book direct. We work on Google Hotels, the website, exclusive direct-channel perks and your past-guest database.

What strategy do you recommend for the green season?

Focus on domestic tourism and specific dates: long weekends, mid-year vacations and midweek getaways. We prepare value-added packages rather than just lowering the rate, and promote them well in advance.

Do you work with small or boutique hotels?

Yes, it’s the type of hotel we work with most. A boutique hotel competes on its story, its location and its experience, and that comes across very well with good photography, content and reviews.

How long until we see results?

Paid campaigns can generate inquiries within the first weeks. Organic Google positioning usually takes three to six months to consolidate. In the proposal we set goals for each stage.

Do I need a booking engine on my website?

It’s ideal for capturing direct bookings at any hour. If you already have one, we connect and optimize it. If not, we recommend options based on your size, or we start with WhatsApp bookings.

How many of your bookings could be direct?

We review your channels, your Google presence and your seasons, and send you a proposal with clear priorities for your hotel.
