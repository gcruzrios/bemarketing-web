The challenge for restaurants

Where tables and orders are lost

A restaurant can have great food and still have empty tables if it doesn’t show up at the moment people decide where to eat.

01

Empty on weekdays, packed on weekends

Fixed costs are the same all seven days, but most of the revenue is concentrated in three.

What we do: we create reasons to visit on weekdays: themed nights, business lunches and day-specific promotions.

02

Orders lost to commissions

Delivery apps bring volume, but they keep a large share of every order and the customer’s contact details.

What we do: we drive direct orders via WhatsApp or your website, with an incentive for those who order direct.

03

Not showing up in “restaurants near me”

If your Google listing has outdated hours, few photos or no menu, the customer picks the place next door.

What we do: we optimize your Google profile with menu, photos, hours, a Reserve button and replied-to reviews.

Frequently asked questions

Restaurant FAQs

How can I attract more customers on weekdays?

By giving a concrete reason to go that day: a themed night, a business lunch or a fixed promotion. Then we promote it to people who live or work nearby, in the days and hours leading up to it.

Is it worth staying on delivery apps?

Usually yes, because they bring new customers. The strategy is to use the apps for discovery and the direct channel for repeat orders: every customer who orders direct the second time leaves you a better margin.

What does the food photography session include?

Photos and short videos of your main dishes, drinks, kitchen, team and atmosphere, with professional lighting and in formats for Google, the digital menu, Instagram and Reels.

How do you handle negative reviews?

We reply quickly and professionally, offering a solution when appropriate. A good public reply shows future customers how your restaurant treats people.

Does it help if my restaurant is new?

Yes. For a new restaurant the priorities are the Google profile, a photo library and the first reviews. We also plan the launch or reopening.
