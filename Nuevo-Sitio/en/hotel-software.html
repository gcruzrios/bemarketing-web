FAQ

Questions from hoteliers

Does it work for small hotels or only chains?

It's designed for boutique hotels, lodges and independent properties from 4 to 150 rooms. Groups with several properties get a consolidated dashboard.

Does it sync with Booking, Expedia and Airbnb?

Yes, via a channel manager: availability and rates update across all channels to prevent overbooking.

Can I take payments online?

Yes. The booking engine and WhatsApp assistant send payment or deposit links through local and international gateways.
